Mapping & Analyzing Participation

Who shows up to build a community? Which organizations sustain engagement over years, and which sectors are consistently absent? This platform answers those questions using {{ total }} attendance records from 42 Tutor/Mentor Leadership and Networking Conferences hosted by Daniel F. Bassill between 1994 and 2015.

Raw attendance sheets are transformed through a documented pipeline — normalization, network construction, and visualization — so findings can be reproduced, extended, and applied to future events. The methodology is explicit at every stage.

Pipeline Overview

Stage 2
Normalization

Stage 3
Network Construction
Attendance becomes nodes and edges — exportable for Kumu or Gephi.

Visualization
Network maps and dashboards make participation patterns visible and interpretable.
