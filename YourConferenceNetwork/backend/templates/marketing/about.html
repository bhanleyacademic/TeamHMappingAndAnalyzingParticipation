About YourConferenceNetwork

Built from 20+ years of real conference participation data.

THE ORIGIN

Built on Real Methodology

YourConferenceNetwork grew out of analysis of 42 Tutor/Mentor Leadership and Networking Conferences held in Chicago between 1994 and 2015 — 6,410 individual attendance records across 2,179 unique organizations.

The methodology maps who shows up to build community. Not just counts, but networks: which organizations co-attended, which sectors were consistently present or absent, and how those patterns shifted over two decades.

We've packaged this methodology so any conference organizer, foundation, or community builder can apply the same network analysis to their own attendance data.

The Sectors

Every organization is mapped to one of 11 canonical sectors: Program, Resource, College, Intermediary, Business, T/MC, Government, K-12 School, Faith, Foundation, or Other.

The normalization pipeline handles typos, compound categories, and case variants automatically — so "program/faith" and "Program-Faith" both map cleanly to "Program".

The Networks

Three graph models answer different questions: the bipartite org–event model asks who attended what; the co-attendance model asks who attended together; the three-layer model asks how events connect sectors and organizations.

The Tools

Export Kumu.io blueprints for interactive web-based network visualization, Gephi CSV files for academic network analysis, or use our built-in interactive charts to explore participation trends over time.
