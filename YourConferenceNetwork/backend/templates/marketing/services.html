What YourConferenceNetwork Does

The full methodology — from CSV upload to publishable network maps.

STEP 1

Upload Your Attendance Data

Download our pre-formatted CSV template. Fill in attendance records from your conference sign-in sheets, registration databases, or event management tools.

Required fields: Organization name, Sector type, Conference date (MM/YYYY), and Location. Optional: Individual names, titles, contact details.

Our pipeline automatically normalizes your sector categories — mapping variants like "Program/Faith" and "program-faith" to a single canonical "Program" category — and parses conference dates into sortable year/month values.

STEP 2

Explore the Dashboard

Once your data is processed, your dashboard shows summary KPIs: total records, unique organizations, unique conference events, and year range.

The stacked bar chart shows attendance per conference event broken down by sector. Filter by year, state, or sector type to focus on specific slices of your data.

Search any organization name to see their full conference history — which events they attended and their sector classification.

Org–Event Bipartite Network

Organizations connected to the conference events they attended. Node size encodes years participated (orgs) and total attendees (events). Filter by minimum events attended.

Export formats: Kumu.io JSON blueprint, Gephi CSV edges

Co-Attendance Network

Organizations connected when they attended the same conference. Edge weight = number of shared conferences. Reveals alliances and community clusters across sectors.

Export formats: Kumu.io JSON blueprint, Gephi CSV edges (weighted)

Three-Layer Directed Network

Conference Events → Sectors → Organizations. A directed three-layer model showing how event types connect to different community sectors and the organizations within them.

Export formats: Kumu.io JSON blueprint, CSV edge list
