Origin Story

From Tutor/Mentor Connection
to Open-Source Platform

From 1994 to 2015, Daniel F. Bassill hosted 42 Tutor/Mentor Leadership and Networking Conferences in Chicago, bringing together nonprofits, government agencies, corporations, universities, and community organizations working to support youth mentoring programs.

Over those two decades, more than 22,000 attendance records accumulated — a rich dataset of who attended, which organizations sent representatives, and how participation changed as the network grew. The data was there. But making sense of it required tools that didn't exist for nonprofits at this scale.

This platform was built to analyze that dataset — and immediately, a broader question emerged: how many other organizations are sitting on the same kind of data, with no way to visualize it?

YourConference.Network is our answer. The entire analysis engine is open source and free to self-host. Any nonprofit, association, or coalition that tracks attendance across recurring events can use it to understand their network — who shows up, who connects with whom, and where the gaps are.
